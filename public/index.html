How to read the labels

Payment failure, a deliberate break, and an unexplained missed cycle have different product levers. App inactivity alone does not establish a missed payment.

Source hygiene

Composites are marked. Help pages describe product rules. Row counts are not unique-user counts or population prevalence.

Opportunity comparison

Directional judgments, not predicted uplift

Score = 100 × corpus share × judged influence × recovery gap × addressability. All rows, including composites and help pages, affect the ranking. The weights are product judgments.

EVIDENCE & EVALUATION

Show the result.
Keep its limits in view.

The 20-item development set checks the original rules baseline. Live AI and the profile-aware journey are separate implementations; this score does not measure their quality.

What is implemented here?

A browser-local editable profile, conversational guidance, education activities, deterministic payment simulations, and optional local check-ins. With a server API key, guidance and research classification use live AI.

Without a key, preview mode uses clearly labelled authored routing. AI errors do not silently turn into preview replies.

Data and control

The saved profile and reminders remain in this browser. In live mode, the profile, recent conversation, and your question are sent to the provider only after consent. Requests use store: false. Provider retention rules can still apply.

No server account, brokerage connection, background notification service, payment execution, or production retention result is claimed.
